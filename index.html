From large scale murals spanning over 500 Square feet, to fine art paintings and clothing design, Geometry was founded by artist Gabriel Ostapchuk in 2025. Having painted in over 4 countries and working with mural clients such as the Squamish Arts Council, Bass Coast Music Festival, private businesses and homeowners, Gabriel has an extensive skill set and high level of professionalism.

He has participated in gallery exhibits displaying his fine art paintings in galleries such as the Outsiders and Others gallery in Vancouver, BC and the BassCamp Gallery at the Shambhala Music Festival. Utilizing a combination of spray paint, brushes, markers and other tools, Gabriel creates bold, vibrant, abstract and surreal works.
